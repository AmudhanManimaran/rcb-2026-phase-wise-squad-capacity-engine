How the plan is built

T_base = λ · VenuePar + (1 − λ) · Capacity
T_recommended = T_base × (2 − BSI)
R_p = T × s_p (venue phase share)
Cost = |CumRuns − CumTarget| + w · E[Wickets]

The dynamic programme picks an intent multiplier per phase (0.80x to 1.25x of adjusted capacity). Higher intent adds runs but raises expected wickets (elasticity 2.2), and wickets above the baseline reduce the scoring resource of later phases.
